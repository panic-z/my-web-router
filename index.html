Welcome to CyberShiba

This is the shared entry point. You can head into the AI news hub, open the resume builder, launch the video downloader, or use the support link below.

One place for AI updates and practical browser tools

CyberShiba helps you monitor AI news and research, jump into a markdown resume builder, download public videos, and access lightweight tools like Top or Flop without creating an account first.

  • AI Info tracks research, engineering posts, and product launches from leading labs.
  • Resume Maker lets you draft in Markdown, preview templates live, and export polished resumes as PDF or HTML.
  • Top or Flop helps you rank options visually and share the board as an image.
  • Video Downloader analyzes public Bilibili and YouTube links and offers available formats.

Frequently asked questions

What is CyberShiba for?
It is the shared entry point for AI Info, Resume Maker, and support, so visitors can discover the right tool without hunting across separate sites.
What can I do in AI Info?
AI Info curates AI news, research, engineering posts, and other updates from major labs, companies, and communities.
What can I do in Resume Maker?
Resume Maker is a browser-based markdown resume builder with live preview, templates, style controls, and one-click export.
What can I do in Top or Flop?
Top or Flop lets you create a visual ranking board, move items into order, and export the result as an image.
What can I do in Video Downloader?
Video Downloader checks public Bilibili and YouTube URLs, shows available formats, and creates a downloadable file.
